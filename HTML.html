About Us

Welcome to bakedcharmm! "Fresh, delicious bakery treats made right at home."

Our Story

Bakedcharmm started in a small home kitchen with one simple recipe and a big love for chocolate.

Today every brownie, fudge slice and cookie is still baked by hand in small batches, using real cocoa, organic butter and ingredients from local dairy farms. Each order is packed with care so it reaches you fresh.

Our 3-Step Baking Promise

  1. We use 100% real cocoa and organic butter.
  2. Everything is baked fresh daily in small batches.
  3. We source our ingredients from local dairy farms.
